## Target titles to search when the time comes
- Senior Backend Engineer / Senior Software Engineer, Backend
- Senior/Staff Backend Engineer, Infrastructure (or "Platform")
- Senior Backend Engineer, Data Platform / Data Engineering (given the Glue/Kafka background)
- Staff Software Engineer, Distributed Systems / Real-Time Systems
- Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) roles will also fit — resilience/game-day/multi-region
  experience is squarely SRE-adjacent, worth not filtering these out later.

## What's commonly required at this level (from current NYC listings)
- Strong Java (or Go — increasingly common alongside Java in infra-heavy NYC postings) and one
  major cloud provider (AWS dominant in NYC fintech).
- Distributed systems fundamentals: Kafka, Kubernetes, Cassandra/DynamoDB-style stores,
  sometimes Flink/Spark for stream processing.
- Infra-as-code (Terraform) and observability tooling (Datadog and similar) — worth having a
  one-line answer ready even if it's not your primary strength, since it shows up in most postings.
- Leadership/scope signals for senior+: architecture ownership, cross-team collaboration,
  mentoring — all things your migration/game-day experience already demonstrates; make sure
  your resume bullets say so explicitly rather than only describing the technical work.

## Comp reference points (NY, as of mid-2026 — re-check before negotiating)
- General senior backend engineer, NY: ~$139K-$192K base (median ~$163K).
- Fintech-specific senior backend: ~$132K-$225K+ base depending on employer tier.
- Production Kafka/distributed-systems depth specifically adds a **$10K-$18K premium** at senior
  level per market data — name this experience explicitly and early in any conversation, it's a
  differentiator, not a footnote.
- Top-end infra/distributed-systems specialists at competitive employers: reports of $300K+ total
  comp — this is the tail, not the median; treat as aspirational context, not a baseline expectation.
- JPMC promotion to Level III plus the insourcing project landing on your resume both strengthen
  your positioning here — re-visit this section after both land, your target range likely moves up.
